▮ THE PROBLEM WHY THIS EXISTS
▮ context customer marketing · n8n

The raw material exists.

The n8n community has 650,000+ active builders sharing workflows, describing automations they've shipped, and explaining problems they've solved. That's an unusually rich reservoir of authentic customer proof — the kind of material most B2B companies spend months trying to surface through formal case study programmes.

But community posts don't become customer stories automatically. Without a system, discovery is manual and reactive: someone stumbles across a post, forwards it in Slack, and it goes nowhere. High-value candidates get missed. The pipeline stays empty.

▮ the gap

Customer marketing is only as good as its sourcing layer. Build the sourcing layer first, and everything else — case studies, testimonials, references — follows from it.

▮ THE THINKING WHY THIS WORKFLOW
▮ rationale customer marketing · n8n

Customer marketing only works if the sourcing layer is reliable. You can build the best case study template in the world — it doesn't matter if there's no repeatable way to find candidates.

n8n is unusual because its customers are builders who already write about what they build. The community forum is a live feed of exactly the kind of proof a customer marketing function needs. The job is to route that signal into a system.

This workflow does one thing: it turns a manual, reactive search into a weekly structured process. Every Monday, high-potential posts land in a tracker. The customer marketing team starts the week with candidates, not a blank page.
